## 1ScreenHGSS v0.3

Play **Pokémon HeartGold / SoulSilver** on a single screen, now with a patcher for
Windows, macOS and Linux.

Whatever you need ends up on the **top** screen, automatically. The battle commands,
Yes / No questions, the X menu, shops and the PC are drawn onto the scene in the game's
own words and font, and everything else is rerouted. Nothing is timed: there is no delay
to wait through and no press that moves the screen out from under you.

### Getting started

Download the patcher for your system, drop your own ROM dump on the window and press
**Patch**. Nothing else to install. The patched file appears next to your original, which
is never modified.

| system | the window | the command line |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | `1ScreenHGSS-0.3.exe` | `1screenhgss-cli-0.3-windows-x86_64.exe` |
| macOS, Apple silicon | `1ScreenHGSS-0.3-macos-arm64.zip` | `1screenhgss-cli-0.3-macos-arm64.tar.gz` |
| macOS, Intel | `1ScreenHGSS-0.3-macos-x86_64.zip` | `1screenhgss-cli-0.3-macos-x86_64.tar.gz` |
| Linux | `1ScreenHGSS-0.3-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | `1screenhgss-cli-0.3-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The macOS app is not signed with an Apple developer ID. The first time, Control-click it
and choose **Open**; on macOS 15, try to open it once, then click **Open Anyway** in
System Settings → Privacy & Security. The command-line versions take the same arguments
as `patch.py`.

Then show only the top screen in your emulator - in melonDS,
`Config → Screen sizing → Top only`. Saves are ordinary battery saves and work on the
unpatched ROM too, so you can move between the two freely.

### What's new in 0.3

**A patcher for every system.** The window and a command-line version, for Windows,
macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Linux.

**Battles**

- The **Safari Zone, the Bug Contest, Pal Park and the first rival battle** get their own
  commands drawn on the scene - BALL / BAIT / MUD / RUN, BALL in place of BAG, FIGHT
  alone - chosen by battle type, the way the game chooses them.
- A **double battle's** second Pokémon shows **CANCEL** instead of RUN, as the touch screen
  does.
- The **selected band keeps the theme's colour.** It no longer flickers back to the
  game's salmon or white in battles, or sticks on white after an evolution.

**Menus and lists**

- **NPC lists open on the top screen**, in the game's own list window, instead of taking
  the screen: fossil revival, the apricorn juice stand, the Pokéathlon Dome, drink
  machines, the Game Corner's prizes, the day-care, the Battle Frontier's prize counters
  and its move tutors, and most one-off NPC choices.
- The **X menu** is drawn on the world in the Safari Zone, the Bug Contest, Pal Park and
  the Union Room too, each in its own layout, and choosing **RETIRE** keeps the world on
  top.
- The **Pal Pad** and the **rankings** open on the top screen. The Union Room's group
  drawing stays on the touch screen, where it can be drawn on.

**Oak's speech**

- The band behind his selected answer is steady, and the ends of his longest lines are no
  longer erased (English).

**L + R**

- The manual swap no longer leaks into the game. It used to turn a bag pocket or a
  Pokédex page, or, with the *L=A* button mode, choose FIGHT in a battle before the screens
  swapped.

**Under the hood**

- Rebuilt against the full decompilation of HeartGold/SoulSilver, which confirmed the
  patch's offsets and corrected a handful. The patch no longer reads memory the game has
  already freed (the bag, the party and the catch-naming screen).
- The patch's resident code is tested under an ARM emulator on every push.

### Tested

Played through on **French SoulSilver** in melonDS: Oak's speech, ordinary wild and
trainer battles, evolutions and their move questions, the X menu, saving, shops, the PC,
drink machines, the day-care, field Yes / No, the Pokédex, PC boxes, Fly, Dig and Escape
Rope, the Pokégear, the opening cinematic and L + R.

Built and checked against the game's code, but **not yet played**: the Safari Zone, Bug
Contest, Pal Park and first-rival battle menus, double battles, the X menu in those zones
and the Union Room, the Battle Frontier lists and move tutors, the Game Corner, Mom's
savings, the Pal Pad and the rankings. If you play any of them, please say how it went.

| game code | game | status |
|---|---|---|
| `IPGF` | SoulSilver (France) | **play-tested on 0.3**, the reference build |
| `IPKF` | HeartGold (France) | play-tested on 0.2b |
| `IPKE` | HeartGold (USA) | play-tested on 0.2b |
| other `IPGx` / `IPKx` | HGSS, other languages | untested, with a good chance of working |

Nothing is hardcoded per region: the patcher finds code by instruction shape and reads
addresses from the ROM's own literals.

### Known limitations

- A few screens stay on the touch screen because they genuinely need the stylus: the
  trainer-card signature, the Ruins of Alph puzzles, the Pokéathlon and the Union Room's
  group drawing. Press **L + R** to reach them.
- A list that would land on a box the script already shows (Mom's savings), or whose
  entries use formatting the patch does not know, still swaps to the touch screen.
- L or R pressed **alone** reaches the game about a tenth of a second late, while the patch
  waits to see whether the other one follows.
- Inside the PC box, L + R does nothing: the box sets its own screens every frame. Press B
  to leave it first.

### Notes

This repository contains no game data. It is a patcher that operates on a dump you
provide of a game you own.
